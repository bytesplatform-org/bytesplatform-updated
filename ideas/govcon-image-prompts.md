# Government contractors page: card image prompts (ChatGPT)

Six images for the "What government buys, what you get" grid on
`/government-contractors`. Each prompt below is complete on its own: paste it
into ChatGPT (image generation) one at a time.

**After generating:** download, compress to roughly 200–300 KB (e.g. Squoosh,
JPEG quality ~75, 1800×1200 or 1536×1024), and save over the file name shown,
in `public/govcon/`. No code changes are needed. The images now sit straight on
the page's pale lavender-grey background (no card box), so light, airy images
with one blue accent fit best.

---

## 1. `websites.jpg`: Website & digital service modernization

```
Create a photorealistic landscape image, 3:2 aspect ratio (1536×1024).

Scene: a city employee's desk in a bright, modern municipal office. A laptop shows a clean, accessible city government website: large clear headings, a big search bar, high-contrast buttons in electric blue (#2457FF), simple icons for services like "pay a bill" and "report an issue" — but render all on-screen text as soft, unreadable shapes. Beside it, a smartphone shows the same site's mobile version. A printed accessibility checklist with tick marks lies next to the keyboard, slightly out of focus.

Style: editorial photography, soft natural daylight from a window, shallow depth of field, calm and premium. Palette of cool off-white (#F0F1FA), light grey and graphite with electric blue as the only accent. Clean, uncluttered, subject slightly off-centre with negative space.

Do not include: readable text or words, real logos, government seals, flags, faces.
```

## Tips

- If ChatGPT adds words anyway, reply: "Same image, but remove all text and lettering."
- To keep the six consistent, generate them in one chat and after the first one say: "Use the same lighting, colour palette and style for the next images."
- Mid-level texture and detail shows the liquid hover ripple best; avoid very flat or very dark results.
